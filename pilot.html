The question this answers

Can your workflow receive plausible market data, execute its own rules correctly, and still act on evidence that is stale, incomplete, shifted, structurally impossible, or materially different from a separate reference?

If every check agrees, the report says the control was redundant on the measured path. If a discrepancy appears, it names the exact dates, fields, thresholds, source relationship, and decision it changed.

What you provide

  • One historical or paper-trading path.
  • The JSON or CSV bars the workflow actually used.
  • A reference series for the same instrument and dates, or permission to use a named public reference.
  • The source names, adjustment conventions, and threshold your team considers material.
  • One technical owner who can classify discrepancies as confirmed, expected, false positive, or unresolved.

Do not send API keys, brokerage credentials, account numbers, customer identities, strategy code, signing secrets, or unrelated positions. Synthetic identifiers are enough.

What Nobulex checks

CheckMeasured failure
IdentityThe returned instrument is not the requested instrument.
Window and completenessThe returned series is materially shorter than the reference, or its dates overlap too little to compare safely.
FreshnessThe newest observation is stale or implausibly in the future.
OrderingDates are duplicated or non-monotonic.
OHLC structureOpen, high, low, and close cannot all be true together.
Reference fidelityClosing prices differ beyond a threshold fixed before the run.
PolicyThe proposed action exceeds a deterministic limit.

What you receive

  1. A decision packet for every governed observation.
  2. A review queue for every would-block and would-escalate result.
  3. A fault-replay report covering seeded stale, truncated, shifted, future-dated, and materially altered data.
  4. A false-positive review completed with your technical owner.
  5. Measured coverage and added latency.
  6. A final recommendation: redundant, continue observing, or test bounded enforcement.

Success criteria

  • Production behavior changes: zero.
  • Coverage of the selected path: reported explicitly.
  • Every non-permit result has a human-readable reason and retained evidence.
  • Customer and Nobulex agree on the count of confirmed issues, expected differences, false positives, and unresolved cases.
  • Every confirmed discrepancy becomes a reusable regression fixture.

The claim boundary

A comparison is independent only when the subject and reference do not share the same upstream truth source. Two vendor names do not prove independence. If lineage is unknown, the report says unknown. If both sides share an upstream, the result is a transport comparison and is labeled that way.

Nobulex currently has no production customer deployment. This offer is designed to produce the first measured customer result, including a negative result if the method adds no information.
